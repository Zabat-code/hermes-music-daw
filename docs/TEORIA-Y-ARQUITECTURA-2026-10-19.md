# Theory and architecture of the genre composer — 2026-10-19

## Epistemic limitations
A musical label is not a necessary and sufficient set of notes. Tradition, era, scene, production, voice, and reception all matter. The profiles below must treat traits as **operating contracts for a defined variant**, not as “the definition of the entire genre.” Umbrella labels (`folk`, `alternative`, `electronic`, `experimental`, `classical`, `comedy_music`, `spoken_word`, and related labels) require a variant/reference and cannot be certified with instrumental MIDI alone. GM MIDI instrumentation does not model strumming, palm mute, frottoir timbre, vocals, 808s, or production; never claim sonic fidelity without listening.

## Musical research and implications for the engine
1. **Form at multiple scales**: Louboutin and Bimbot (ISMIR 2017) study chord-sequence structure and recurrences linking distant segments. A chord-to-chord Markov model lacks formal memory; first generate A/B/chorus and **bring back a motif** in distant sections. Original source: https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2017/paper/000070.pdf
2. **Harmonic functions are not universal**: Open Music Theory distinguishes tonic prolongation, preparation, and dominant/cadential function in tonal repertoire. Duinker (MTO 2019) explains open/ambiguous pop loops that do not depend on V–I. Include distinct strategies accordingly: `functional`, `loop`, `modal`, `blues_12`, `jazz_cycle`, `vamp`, `drone`; do not force a tonal cadence onto every genre. Sources: https://openmusictheory.github.io/harmonicSyntax1.html and https://www.mtosmt.org/issues/mto.19.25.4/mto.19.25.4.duinker.html
3. **Percussion also has form**: Geary (MTO 2024) shows the formal functions of changes in drum patterns and density. The organizer must label density/energy/transitions rather than merely permuting chords. Source: https://mtosmt.org/issues/mto.24.30.2/mto.24.30.2.geary.html
4. **Metal**: Boddington/Jordan/Herbst analyzed 100 songs across five subgenres (power, black, metalcore, melodic death, progressive). They report a contrast between Dorian treatment/dominant colors in *their power metal sample* and non-diatonic minor chords in black metal. Garza (MTO 2021) distinguishes riff and cymbal types to explain meter interpretation: BPM alone is insufficient. Sources: https://pure.hud.ac.uk/en/publications/harmonic-structures-in-twenty-first-century-metal-music-a-harmoni and https://mtosmt.org/issues/mto.21.27.1/mto.21.27.1.garza.php
5. **Jazz, samba/bossa**: Antonio J. García distinguishes swing ride/hi-hat performance and different partido alto cells for samba/bossa; this requires multiple beats and articulation, not 16 steps of pop drums. https://garciamusic.com/educator/articles/swing.bossa.samba.drum.html
6. **Afro-Caribbean music**: Smithsonian Folkways teaches 3-2/2-3 patterns and bells in multi-bar cycles; do not blindly choose 3-2 or 2-3 without establishing the tradition. https://folkways.si.edu/braiding-rhythms-the-role-of-bell-patterns-in-west-african-and-afro-caribbean-music/tools-for-teaching/smithsonian
7. **Other families**: original Ableton material shows breakbeat/DnB with breaks and sub-bass, and footwork producers describe their practices. Smithsonian Folkways presents the structure/instruments of zydeco, mariachi, bluegrass, tango, and calypso. Instituto Andaluz del Flamenco describes the metres of flamenco styles: 12 beats are not equivalent to pop 4/4. Sources: https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/dnb-beats-and-sub-bass-tutorial-dj/ ; https://folkways.si.edu/lesson/instruments-of-cajun-and-zydeco ; https://folkways.si.edu/bluegrass-folkways-american-roots-tradition/history/music/article/smithsonian ; https://folkways.si.edu/dance-traditions-argentina/chacarera-tango/music/tools-for-teaching/smithsonian ; https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/aaiicc/flamenco/content/cantes-de-comp%C3%A1s-mixto

## Proposed algorithm: hierarchical grammar with bounded permutation
- Parameters: genre + variant, key/mode, energy, bars per section, reproducible seed, separate instrumental arrangement.
- Select the profile's **harmonic strategy**; build a 2/4/8/12-bar motif A (12 as the blues exception), and store its functional signature.
- Generate a form plan (intro/A/B/A'/bridge/A''/coda) with tension curves, harmonic rhythm, and section roles. `A'` preserves motif anchors and changes **at most one functional segment/ornament**; the bridge allows greater harmonic distance and recovers an anchor for the return. Do not randomly permute every chord.
- Choose transformations using constraints (mode/scale, function, optional cadence, smooth root motion, and common tones), penalties (mechanical repetition, cadences outside the idiom, three identical leaps, overly frequent chord changes, excessive novelty), and rewards for motif identity/appropriate contrast. Explicit heuristic weights, not claims of human taste.
- Generate chord events with actual durations (one per bar or, where appropriate, two per bar); retain symbols and degrees, along with motifs/permutations/functions and decision provenance. A 16-step pop grid does not represent clave/compound meter/triple meter.
- **Honesty gate**: automated structural checks; listening tests with references and musicians are still pending. Profiles document minimum requirements and subvariants, but harmony alone does not make a genre sound authentic.

## Source policy
Assign each profile an `evidence_level`: `direct` (original source on that genre), `family` (original source for a family), or `provisional` (musical hypothesis for testing). Never promote it to `verified_audio` without audio comparison and human acceptance. Maintain specific sources per profile and a list of gaps requiring research.
